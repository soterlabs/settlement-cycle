# MSC #14 - Settlement Summary (October 2026)

> Draft settlement instructions and TMF recommendations; execution remains subject to approval.

## Methodology

Demand-side revenue covers incentives and treasury income. Supply-side revenue covers allocation returns, borrowing costs and Sky Direct Exposures.

October-earned revenue is shown separately from adjustments. Instructions include [Reconciliation IV](reconciliation-2026-01-to-2026-09.md) once, together with the other adjustments shown below. Final mint and transfer amounts are rounded to whole USDS after combining full-precision components. Potential discrepancies can be corrected in a subsequent reconciliation.

Sources: [October reports](../settlements), [reconciliation ledger](../reconciliation/2026-01_to_2026-09/data.json) and [instruction preview](../reconciliation/2026-10/prime_msc_preview.json). No payment has been executed by this calculation.
