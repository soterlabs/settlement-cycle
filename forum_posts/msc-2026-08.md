# MSC #12 - Settlement Summary (August 2026)

## Methodology

The Monthly Settlement Cycle (MSC) is a recurring process that synchronizes
core financial operations, governance functions, and risk management
activities across the ecosystem.

- **Demand Side Primitives** - Incentives to Prime Agents to drive USDS adoption.
- **Supply Side Primitives** - Borrowing USDS collateral at the Base Rate from
  Sky to generate risk-adjusted returns, under the restrictions of the Asset
  Liability Management framework.

*Note: Potential inaccuracies will be corrected in future MSCs, per Atlas
governance.*
