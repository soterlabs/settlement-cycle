# MSC #13 - Settlement Summary (September 2026)

## Methodology

The Monthly Settlement Cycle (MSC) is a recurring process that synchronizes core financial operations, governance functions, and risk management activities across the ecosystem.

- **Demand Side Primitives** - Incentives to Prime Agents to drive USDS adoption.
- **Supply Side Primitives** - Borrowing USDS collateral at the Base Rate from Sky to generate risk-adjusted returns, under the restrictions of the Asset Liability Management framework.

*Note 1: Potential inaccuracies will be corrected in future MSCs, per Atlas governance.*

*Note 2: September calculations are sourced from the [settlement reports on main](https://github.com/soterlabs/settlement-cycle/tree/536f1492089ed82fbf6eaabb956f7a194b887d47/settlements), pinned to commit [`536f149`](https://github.com/soterlabs/settlement-cycle/commit/536f1492089ed82fbf6eaabb956f7a194b887d47).*

The settlement instructions below include the adjustments in the [January–August 2026 reconciliation](TBD). September-earned revenue is presented separately from these historical corrections. Final mint and transfer amounts are rounded to whole USDS after adding the adjustments. Skybase's historical correction assumes no payments for these items outside the published MSC reports; any confirmed off-report payments must be deducted before settlement.
