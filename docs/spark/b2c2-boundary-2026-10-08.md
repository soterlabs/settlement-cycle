# Spark B2C2 entrypoint capital

The [November 27, 2025 spell](https://github.com/sparkdotfi/spark-spells/blob/d67876686f82656710ca1baa352ddab65d49907d/archive/20251127/SparkEthereum_20251127.sol)
authorizes USDC, USDT and PYUSD transfers to
`0xa29e963992597b21bcdcaa969d571984869c4ff5`, explicitly identified as B2C2.
The actual Ethereum ALM boundary has ten transfers through the August replay pin:

| Flow | Asset | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Initial test payment | USDC | 100 |
| Test returns | USDC + USDT | 10 + 10 |
| Further test payment | USDT | 5 |
| April 22 payment / return | USDC / USDT | 100,000 / 100,000 |
| April 22 payment / return | USDC / USDT | 1,000,000 / 999,314 |
| April 22 payment / return | USDC / USDT | 1,000,000 / 999,320 |

Total paid: **2,100,105 USD**. Total received: **2,098,654 USD**. All returns
come directly from the same authorized entrypoint to Spark's Ethereum ALM.
This establishes the boundary under the operator's EOA policy without tracing
commingled balances or inferring another wallet's identity.

`src/settle/compute/spark_b2c2_capital.py` assigns the ten reviewed flows to the
analytics-only `S_B2C2_ENTRY` funding account. Deposits carry their actual funding
mix; returns release the same principal across the two par-stable cash tokens.
No new debt or presumed interest is manufactured. The exact transaction,
block, timestamp, asset and normalized cash leg must match the evidence.
Unknown or inconsistent funding fails closed. Ordinary unknown transfers are
not matched by amount/date proximity.

**1,451 USD remains in the diagnostic boundary account**: 85 from onboarding
flows and 1,366 from the two large conversions. This is outstanding funding
under the boundary convention, not proof that B2C2 still owes that amount in
cash. The transfers alone do not distinguish fees from retained cash. It is
not silently written off, assigned an idle exemption, or called a completed
fee reconciliation. A statement or order settlement would close that question.

The canonical fixture contains the complete matched transaction logs. Both
an all-token boundary query and a query restricted to the three authorized
stablecoins returned the same 118 logs. Regression tests validate the ten
actual transfers, basis conservation, cross-token returns, own-money funding,
pinned cutoffs, missing deposits and changed evidence. The adapter also passed
against all ten transactions from the actual saved Spark normalized history,
with every per-ilk debt record unchanged.

This changes capital attribution only. Revenue, published settlement reports,
API output and global borrowing charges are unchanged. Final August costs
still require the full Spark replay and remain subject to its other funding
gaps, including Savings V2.
