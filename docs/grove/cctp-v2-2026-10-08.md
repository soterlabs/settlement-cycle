# Grove CCTP v2 capital tracing

The generic bridge normalizer currently implements **CCTP v1 only**. Grove
upgraded Ethereum and deployed Base controllers with **CCTP v2** in its
[January 15 Ethereum spell](https://github.com/grove-labs/grove-spells/blob/97bbdf8d89e824e93a28ecaf9cf0628ebc640d12/archive/20260115/GroveEthereum_20260115.sol)
and [Base spell](https://github.com/grove-labs/grove-spells/blob/97bbdf8d89e824e93a28ecaf9cf0628ebc640d12/archive/20260115/GroveBase_20260115.sol).
The missing v2 links left real USDC burns and subsequent mints disconnected.

## Evidence and scope

The historical audit finds 77 completed messages through the August pin:

| Direction | Messages | USDC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ethereum → Base | 62 | 314,595,379.874274 |
| Base → Ethereum | 15 | 34,008,407.000000 |

One source transaction sends two messages ($10m and $5m); each payment is
consumed separately. All audited messages use standard finality (2000), zero
fees and no hooks. This patch links those exact reviewed messages; it does
not claim general future CCTP v2 extraction coverage.

V2 cannot reuse v1's nonce matcher. Its source `MessageSent` contains an empty
nonce; Circle assigns it off-chain. The audit queried Circle's transaction-
scoped `/v2/messages/{sourceDomain}?transactionHash=…` endpoint. For every
message the tests check:

- Actual source USDC payment to TokenMinter and burn, without payment reuse.
- Source MessageTransmitter and TokenMessenger emitters, complete source
  message template and its transaction/block identity.
- Circle's nonce binding and attested bytes against that source template.
- Destination MessageReceived nonce, source domain, token messenger, full
  burn-message body, mint recipient, amount, token and finality.
- The corresponding actual destination USDC mint and zero fee.

Contract addresses and message semantics follow [Circle's contract reference](https://developers.circle.com/cctp/references/contract-addresses)
and [message implementation](https://github.com/circlefin/evm-cctp-contracts/blob/a92a2b4e7e6ef99bf0b05dca71780f5ec190e729/src/messages/v2/MessageV2.sol).
The attestation was accepted by the destination transmitter; the fixture tests
validate the resulting logs rather than reimplementing signature recovery.
All canonical logs and Iris responses are committed in
`tests/fixtures/grove_cctp_v2_proofs.json`. No amount/date heuristic is used.

Pending bridge cash retains its borrowed/earned funding mix and is included
under the originating cash venue until received. An incomplete pinned history
keeps the claim outstanding. Missing funding, changed amounts/blocks or mixing
raw and already linked routes fail. No new debt is created by a destination mint.

Neither ilk is fully reconciled. The BLOOM-A bounds remain broad. Unknown
funding earlier in the history still affects later positions and prevents the
modeled costs from being reported as validated allocation charges.

Evidence: `reconciliation/grove_cctp_v2_2026_08.json`. All **280** relevant
allocation/Grove tests pass. The preceding broad suite passed **1,546** tests
(two environment-dependent skips); the new v2 tests then exercise every added
route and adverse/missing-input cases. Published reports, API data, total debt
and global borrowing costs are unchanged.
